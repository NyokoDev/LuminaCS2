# Lumina

**Lumina**, formerly known as **ColorAdjustments**, is a comprehensive post-processing mod for **Cities: Skylines II** that gives you complete control over the game's visual presentation.

From simple color grading to advanced HDRP Volume editing, Lumina lets you customize nearly every aspect of the game's rendering pipeline.

Save and load presets at any time, allowing you to switch between different visual styles without recreating your settings every time you launch the game.

## ✨ Features

### 🎨 Visual Adjustments

- Exposure
- Contrast
- Saturation
- Hue Shift
- White Balance
- Shadows, Midtones & Highlights
- Tone Mapping
- LUT Selection
- Cubemap Replacement
- Time of Day Control
- Planetary Settings
- Road Color Adjustment
- Road Wear Texture Replacement
- Import & Export Presets

## 🧩 Lumina NGX — Volume Inspector

**Lumina NGX** is an advanced HDRP Volume Inspector that exposes Unity's **High Definition Render Pipeline (HDRP)** Volume system directly inside Cities: Skylines II.

With NGX you can:

- Browse every HDRP Volume in the scene
- Add or remove Volume Components
- Inspect and edit component parameters in real time

> **Lumina NGX is currently in Beta.**

NGX is designed for advanced users who want direct access to Unity's rendering pipeline without leaving the game.
